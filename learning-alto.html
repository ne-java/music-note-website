Alto Clef

The Alto Clef is mainly used by Violas, who are inbetween the range of the Treble Clef and Bass Clef, so musicians decided to create a clef that meets half-way inbetween. Normally, the viola is the only one who reads this clef, though some cellists if needed to go higher than bass, but lower than treble might use the alto clef.

The Alto Clef is sometimes called the C clef, because of the small indent that is on the 3 looking shape, which points to the 3rd line or C.
