Treble Clef

The Treble Clef is mostly used by violins, though violas can still use them, just with a bit of difficulty!

The reason why it the treble clef is sometimes called the g clef, is because the swirl on the inside (do you see it?) points to the 2nd line, which corresponds with the note G!
